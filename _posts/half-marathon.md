Running a half marathon had always felt out of reach for me. I decided to commit to the challenge last year and started following a simple training plan. Three short runs during the week and a longer run every weekend slowly built up my stamina. I learned how important it was to stay consistent, eat well, and take rest days seriously.

The race itself was both exhausting and exhilarating. Crossing the finish line after 13.1 miles was one of the most satisfying feelings I've had. If you're thinking about running a half marathon, start small and keep at it—you might surprise yourself with what you can accomplish.
